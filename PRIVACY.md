# Privacy Policy for Leaf VN Studio

**Last Updated:** October 31, 2026  
**Effective Date:** October 31, 2026  

---

## 1. Overview
Welcome to **Leaf VN Studio** ("we", "our", or "the Engine"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any personal data you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains how our application collects, uses, stores, and protects your information when you access or interact with Leaf VN Studio, including sign-in via third-party providers such as **Facebook Login (Meta Platforms, Inc.)**, **Google Sign-In**, and **GitHub**.

---

## 2. Information We Collect
When you use Leaf VN Studio, we may collect the following categories of information:
* **Authentication & Profile Data:** When signing in with Facebook, Google, or GitHub, we receive basic public profile information (such as your display name, profile avatar, and verified email address) provided by the respective OAuth identity provider.
* **Project & Story Data:** Visual novel scenarios, scripts, dialogue lines, assets (images, audio clips), variables, and configuration metadata created or imported by you.
* **Technical & Device Information:** Standard browser session storage, IndexedDB caches, and language preferences necessary to run the engine locally on your device.

---

## 3. How We Use Your Information
We use the collected information exclusively to:
* Authenticate your identity and provide secure access to your visual novel projects.
* Save and sync your projects with cloud storage (Firebase / Google Drive) upon your request.
* Maintain your personal workspace preferences, UI themes, and audio volume configurations.
* Provide customer support, bug fixes, and security patches.

> **Important:** We **DO NOT** sell, rent, monetize, or share your personal information or visual novel creations with third-party advertisers or data brokers under any circumstances.

---

## 4. Third-Party Services & Authentication
Our application integrates with trusted third-party service providers for authentication and hosting:
* **Meta Platforms, Inc. (Facebook Login):** Used solely to verify your account identity. You can manage or revoke access anytime from your Facebook Account Settings.
* **Google Firebase & Google Cloud:** Used for secure user authentication (Firebase Auth) and database hosting.
* **GitHub, Inc.:** Used as an optional developer sign-in method.

---

## 5. User Data Deletion Instructions (Facebook & OAuth Data)
In compliance with Meta Platform Terms and General Data Protection Regulations (GDPR/CCPA), you have the right to request the complete deletion of all personal data associated with your account.

### How to remove Leaf VN Studio from your Facebook account:
1. Go to your Facebook Profile > **Settings & Privacy > Settings**.
2. In the left menu, select **Apps and Websites**.
3. Find **Leaf VN Studio** and click **Remove**.
4. Confirm deletion.

### How to request full database deletion of your account and cloud saves:
You can delete your cloud-stored projects directly from the in-app Project Manager by clicking the "Delete" icon, or send an email request to our privacy team at:  
📧 **privacy@leafvn.com** or **leafvn.team@gmail.com** with the subject *"Data Deletion Request"*. Your account and associated cloud records will be permanently purged within 30 days.

---

## 6. Data Storage & Security
We implement industry-standard encryption protocols (HTTPS/TLS) for data in transit and secure Firebase authentication standards. Project files stored locally in your browser (IndexedDB) never leave your device unless you explicitly trigger an export or cloud backup.
